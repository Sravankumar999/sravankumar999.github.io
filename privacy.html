Privacy Policy

This app is a personal tool used only by its owner to read and add events on the owner's own Google Calendar.

It accesses Google Calendar data only to check free time and create or remove events requested by the owner. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Nothing is stored beyond what is needed to perform the requested action.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
